When the river finds its way home
A restored floodplain is teaching one small town to make room for the water.
Words by Mira Ellis · Photography by Luca Morgan · 8 minute read
At first, the change was almost invisible. A line of willow cuttings followed an old bend in the river, and the fence that had divided the meadow for a century was gone.
By spring, shallow pools had gathered in the hollows. Herons stood among the reeds. On the path above the water, neighbours stopped to compare what they had seen that morning.
The project began with a simple question: what would happen if the valley could hold rain again? Its answer is neither a monument nor a machine. It is a landscape learning to move.
