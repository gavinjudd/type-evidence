## B — OFFCUT

J/K best meet the tactile, playful, confident studio tone. Their rounded, interrupted title shapes give the word a cut-out, ink-like character. OFFCUT is recognizable in the full and quarter views, with no clipped ink. The steady supporting letterforms let date, location and participation copy stay straightforward beneath the expressive title; this is a useful relationship in the actual composition.

M is a sound neutral alternative. Its larger title has more presence than L’s smaller neutral treatment, so M has a slight advantage over L. Both remain clear and confident, but provide less of the requested independent-studio character than J/K. This ranking does not imply that neutral typography is inherently inferior.

All four date lines are easy to locate beneath the divider at full size. The supporting Polish copy and visible diacritics appear intact, with no visible overlaps. Every poster is 1000 × 1260, within the 1500 px limit; quarter views are 250 × 315.

**Corrections worth making:** Keep the expressive lettering confined to the title in J/K and retain its clear support treatment. If choosing L’s neutral direction, increase its title presence toward M. For an actual social derivative, enlarge the event information relative to the page rather than simply shrinking this poster: quarter views preserve the title well, while smaller instructions become fine print. That is an adaptation issue, not evidence of a clipped-title failure.

**Limit:** This is a subjective tone and hierarchy judgment, not measured memorability. PNGs do not prove Polish coverage by the exact intended face, lack of fallback, licensing or print quality. The title contains only OFFCUT; unused Polish coverage in that role is not a valid reason to reject it.
